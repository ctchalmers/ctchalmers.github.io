Privacy Policy — Cordelia

Effective October 6, 2026

What Cordelia is

Cordelia is a private, single-user application operated by its owner, Taylor Chalmers, for personal use. It is not distributed to or used by anyone else. The only Google account connected to it is the owner's own.

What it accesses

Cordelia requests two read-only Google permissions:

  • Gmail (read-only) — to read the owner's mail when preparing a daily briefing.
  • Google Calendar (read-only) — to read the owner's upcoming events for the same purpose.

It cannot send mail, modify or delete messages, create or change events, or access Google Drive.

How data is handled

Data read from Google is processed on a computer owned and controlled by the owner, for the sole purpose of producing briefings delivered to the owner. It is not sold, shared, transferred, or disclosed to any third party, and it is not used for advertising, profiling, or model training. Authentication tokens are stored only on that computer, protected by file-system permissions, and are never published.

Limited Use

Cordelia's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revocation takes effect immediately.

Contact

Questions about this policy: the owner's address shown on the Google consent screen.
